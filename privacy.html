Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 2026

Who we are

Tempera is a mobile puzzle game published by Carlos Hinostroza (Chile). If you have any questions, contact: katsumi.mizutanip@gmail.com

What information do we collect?

Tempera itself does not collect, store, or share any personal data. The game does not include analytics, crash reporting, advertising networks, social media integration, or account creation.

However, the following data is processed by third-party services integrated into the game:

Service Data Collected Purpose
HUAWEI IAP Device ID, country/region, purchase history, HUAWEI ID Processing in-app purchases

We do not sell your personal information. All payment processing is handled by HUAWEI IAP; we never receive or store payment credentials.

In-App Purchases

Purchases are processed entirely by HUAWEI IAP (Huawei Mobile Services). See HUAWEI's privacy policy at: https://www.huawei.com/en/privacy-policy

Data Retention

We do not store any personal data on our servers. Purchase records are managed by HUAWEI IAP according to their data retention policies.

Third-Party Services

  • HUAWEI IAP — in-app purchases

Children's Privacy

We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 7. If you believe a child has provided us with personal data, contact us and we will delete it.
