Custody Chain

A game about institutional corruption, missing persons,
and the horses that nobody is talking about.

"Three rules. Don't ask what's inside. Don't ask who it was before. And I charge double if there are cats involved."

— V. Reyes, Courier. Probably the last time she said "easy peasy."

01

case files

the setup

Valentina Reyes used to be special forces. Now she's a courier with a reputation: fast, clean, no questions asked.

Her latest job: break into the Ministry of Justice, floor 8, office 812. Grab a hard drive the size of a wallet. Leave.

Sounds simple.

the complication

She opens the drive.

Testimony files from eight missing persons. One colonel's name underlined by hand. And a folder labeled "HRAS LOGISTICA" that somehow involves competition horses.

Eight people. And it's all about horses.

the question

She could walk away. Delete everything. Pretend she never looked.

But Val has a problem: she's the kind of person who opens things she shouldn't.

And now she knows something nobody wanted her to know.

02

how it plays

stealth

Guards have vision cones. Cameras have patterns. Learn them, exploit them, or—if you must—knock them out. Quiet.

sandevistan

When everything goes wrong, time slows down. You get three, maybe four seconds to cross. Use it sparingly. When it runs out, everything changes.

narrative

What you read, what you ignore, what you reveal to others. The story shifts based on what Val knows—and what she pretends not to.

color as story

The palette shifts with the narrative. Cold blues in the ministry. Amber from emergency lights. The red of revelation. You feel the story before you understand it.

03

persons of interest

Valentina Reyes protagonist

"The one who never asks." Ex-special forces. Dry humor. A habit of opening things she shouldn't. You're playing as her.

Héctor Palacios the client

Lawyer in a nice suit. Money, connections, and a very specific list of things he doesn't want to discuss. Hired Val for this job. Knows more than he's saying.

Dr. Ernesto Campos the office

Whose office holds the drive. Family photos on the desk. Airtight security. Doesn't know his secret is already gone.

Colonel Andrés Vidal name from a file

Unit Special Intervention. Mentioned once in the testimony files. Has seventeen competition horses. Nobody is talking about this.
