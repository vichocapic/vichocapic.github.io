what is this

the concept

a cozy survival game about leaving the city behind and building a simple life in the countryside.

farm, gather, build bonds with animals and spirits. a journey of personal healing.

what happened

i started tinyfield with a lot of enthusiasm. the procedural world, biomes, seasons, npc relationships...

the amount of content it required was enormous. i paused it to focus on custody chain.

the future

it's a project i really like. maybe i'll come back to it someday.

the countryside will always be waiting.

the prototype is already playable on the web.
